Track what you spend by texting yourself on WhatsApp.

How it works

You already know the interface. It is your own WhatsApp chat.

The bot listens to one chat only: the conversation you have with yourself. Open WhatsApp, search your own name, and pick “Message yourself”. Anything you type there is read, understood and filed. Nothing else on your phone is touched, and nobody else can send anything to the bot.

Youalmuerzo 1200
Bot✅ $1,200.00 · food · 26 Aug

That green tick is the important part. It is sent only after the row is safely saved in your spreadsheet. If you see a tick, the expense is recorded. If something went wrong, the bot says so in plain words instead.

Categories

You never type a category. The bot chooses one and learns from you.

Each expense is filed under a category so your monthly totals mean something. The bot reads the categories already in your sheet and reuses them wherever it can. When something genuinely new comes along, it creates a category for it.

You are in charge of the list. The categories live in your spreadsheet, not inside the bot. Rename a category in the sheet and the bot follows it from then on. If you would rather it stopped inventing new ones, tidy the column and it will stick to what it finds.

When the bot asks a question

If it cannot tell how much something cost, it asks rather than guessing. Reply with just the number and it remembers what you were talking about.

A message with no expense in it is simply ignored, with a note so you know it was seen and not silently swallowed.

Commands

Five words worth remembering.

CommandWhat it does
undo Removes everything the last message added. If that message logged three expenses, all three go. Tells you exactly what it removed.
total This month’s spending, with a breakdown per category, largest first.
total food This month for one category. Works with any category in your sheet.
clean Empties the sheet completely, keeping the column headings. A backup file is saved first, and your spreadsheet’s own version history is a second safety net.
help A short reminder of the above, plus the categories currently in use.

Totals are not converted between currencies. If you log in both dollars and yen, each is totalled separately and shown side by side. The bot will not invent an exchange rate for you.

Why the last column matters. If the bot ever files something oddly, the original wording is right there next to it. You can correct the row by hand and see immediately what it was working from. The sheet is yours — edit any cell at any time. The bot reads it back the next time you ask for a total.

If something goes wrong

The bot tells you plainly. It never pretends a failed save worked.

MessageWhat it meansWhat to do
⚠️ Sheet write failed… Google Sheets was unreachable. Your expense was saved on the server and is queued. Nothing. It is retried automatically every minute.
⚠️ Could not reach the parser… The language service was briefly unavailable. Nothing was recorded. Send the message again in a moment.
🤔 No expense found… No amount could be found in the message. Include a number, or send help.
No reply at all The service is not running. Contact whoever administers the bot.
A tick always means saved. The confirmation is sent after the write succeeds, never before, so you can trust it. Anything short of a tick means the expense is either queued or not recorded, and the message says which.

Good to know

Dates follow one timezone

“Yesterday” is worked out in the timezone the bot is configured for, not your phone’s. It matters only if you log around midnight while travelling.

Only you can use it

The bot answers messages you send in your own chat and ignores everything else. Nobody can add rows to your sheet by messaging you.

Correct anything by hand

Wrong category or amount? Edit the cell in the spreadsheet. Nothing in the bot needs changing, and totals update immediately.

Not included

Photographs of receipts, voice notes, budgets, recurring expenses and currency conversion are outside what this bot does.

A one-minute start

  • Open WhatsApp and go to the chat with yourself.
  • Send help — the bot replies with what it understands.
  • Send a real expense, for example coffee 4.50.
  • Look at your spreadsheet. The row is already there.
  • Send total at the end of the day.
